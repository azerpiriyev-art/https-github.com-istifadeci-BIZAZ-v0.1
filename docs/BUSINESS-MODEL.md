# Business Model — v0.2 working model

## Default operating model
BIZAZ is a two-sided B2B marketplace/workflow platform:
- Buyers publish purchase requests.
- Qualified suppliers submit offers.
- Buyers compare offers and select suppliers.
- Orders, payments, delivery and reviews create the transaction history.

## Monetization candidates
1. Transaction commission — preferred long-term option; exact rate is a human business decision.
2. Supplier subscription — optional premium tier.
3. Featured supplier/product placement — optional, with disclosure controls.
4. Enterprise workflow subscription — later phase.

The platform architecture supports these without hard-coding a commission rate.

## Trust model
- Company identity: legal name + VÖEN + legal form.
- Role-based company membership.
- Supplier offer history and performance metrics.
- Audit trail for sensitive actions.

## Human decision gates
Before charging real money or launching commercially, the owner must decide/approve: commission/subscription pricing, payment-provider/legal contracting, refund/dispute policy, terms/privacy wording, and tax/legal treatment of platform revenue.
